OWNER REPORTS NOT BORROWED BROCHURES

The review module

Reports built by counting what owners actually discuss, with the sample size, the period and the source links printed on every page — and no borrowed star ratings.

Most equipment "reviews" on the internet are a specification sheet rewritten in the first person by someone who has never sat on the machine. This module exists to do something narrower and more honest: count what actual owners discuss, show the sample, and link to the source.

What a report contains

The sample, stated up front. How many discussions were read, out of how many exist, and over what period. If the sample is small, the report says the findings are directional.

Topic counts, not paraphrased posts. We record what owners keep returning to — an attachment that dominates the conversation, a transmission behavior that comes up repeatedly, the competitor set they actually cross-shop. Every thread is linked so you can read it at the source.

What is absent. In a sample of nineteen discussions with no thread about engine failure, that silence is worth reporting too — carefully, because absence of evidence in a small sample is not evidence of absence.

What we cannot tell you. Every report ends with its own limits. A topic count cannot produce a failure rate, and we will not imply one.

What these reports are not

They are not first-person tests. They do not carry a star rating borrowed from another site. They do not rank machines out of ten.

That last one is deliberate: a single score hides the trade-off, and the trade-off is the only thing worth knowing when two machines in the same class are within a horsepower of each other.

Current reports

The Kubota L2501 owner report is the first, built on 19 discussions from the model's own database. It found grapple selection dominating the conversation, cross-shopping against Kioti and Mahindra rather than John Deere, and buyers explicitly choosing the machine for staying under the emissions threshold.

More will follow model by model, at the pace we can source them properly.

Standing method
  • Every report states how many owner sources were read and over what period.
  • Owners and service technicians are quoted by name where they agreed to it.
  • Where we have hands-on experience ourselves, we say so and show the receipt.
  • Where we do not, we say that too — no invented first-person testing.
