Privacy policy

Updated 16 Aug 2026

This is a publication, not a service. There is no account to create, nothing to log in to, and nothing we ask you to submit. That keeps this policy short and mostly about what does not happen here.

What we collect

Nothing you type. There are no accounts, no newsletter sign-up, no contact form and no comment section. We do not ask for your name, email address or location, so we do not hold them.

Standard server records. Like any website, the hosting provider records requests: IP address, the page requested, the time, the browser's user agent string. These are ordinary web server logs, kept by the host for operational and security purposes.

What our tools keep locally. The calculators on this site run entirely in your browser. The values you enter — ZIP code, acreage, tent size, crop — are processed on your device and are not sent to us or stored on our servers. Some pages remember a preference (such as whether you dismissed a prompt) in your browser's local storage, on your device, where you can clear it at any time.

Analytics and cookies

We do not run advertising networks, and there are no third-party advertising cookies on this site.

Where analytics are in use, they are limited to aggregate traffic measurement — which pages are read, roughly where readers come from — and are not used to build a profile of you or to target advertising at you elsewhere.

Links to other sites

Some links on this site go to sellers and may earn us a commission; they are marked, and the affiliate disclosure explains the arrangement in full.

When you follow one of those links you leave this site, and the destination applies its own privacy policy and its own cookies. A tracking parameter in the link tells the seller which site sent you. We do not receive your name, your payment details or the contents of your order — only, at most, aggregate reporting that a referral converted.

The same applies to any link to a manufacturer, an extension service or a regulator. We have no control over what those sites collect.

Third parties

The site is served as static files from a hosting provider, which processes the server logs described above on our behalf.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any that would be worth selling.

Your choices

You can browse this site with cookies disabled and everything except a couple of remembered preferences will work exactly the same. Clearing your browser's local storage removes anything a page has remembered on your device.

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants you rights over personal data — access, correction, deletion — those rights apply to whatever a data controller holds about you. In our case that is limited to server logs, which are not indexed by identity and cannot be searched by name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. The site is published from a version-controlled repository, so revisions are recorded rather than silently overwritten.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything published here, go through the contact route on the about page.
