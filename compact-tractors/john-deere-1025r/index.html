The 1025R is the machine this class is measured against, mostly because more of them have been sold than anything else with four wheels and a mid-mount deck. Built in Augusta, Georgia since 2013, it sits at the top of Deere's sub-compact range with 23.8 hp at the engine and 18 hp at the PTO.

What the sales figures hide is how narrow the machine is. It lifts 681 lb at the hitch and moves 3.5 GPM to an implement. Those two numbers, not the engine rating, decide what you can hang on it.

What this machine is for

Mowing, snow, moving material in a loader bucket, and light work on a garden-scale plot. With a 54 or 60 inch mid-mount deck it is a very good mower that also happens to have a hitch. That is the honest description of the machine, and it is why so many are sold to people with three acres and a long driveway.

Where it stops is tillage and anything continuous on the hydraulics. 18 PTO hp and 681 lb of lift put a hard ceiling on implement width, and 3.5 GPM is enough for a loader and a hitch cylinder but not for an attachment with a motor running all day.

The lift figure is the whole argument

681 lb at 24 inches behind the hitch is the number that defines this tractor. A five-foot rotary cutter weighs more than that before it touches grass. A six-foot box blade is out of the question. Even a modest rotary tiller sits at the limit, and the PTO rating agrees with the hitch: 18 hp is under what most tillers in that width are specified to need.

This is not a fault, it is a class. The mistake is buying the 1025R for work that needs a compact rather than a sub-compact, then discovering the ceiling after the implement is paid for. If the list of jobs includes ground engagement, the honest comparison is against a machine that lifts twice as much.

What it weighs, and why that matters more here

1,444 lb is light for loader work. The loader will lift material the tractor cannot hold down, which is the mechanism behind every photo of a sub-compact standing on its front wheels. Rear ballast — a filled box blade, wheel weights, a ballast box — is not an accessory on this machine, it is part of the configuration.

John Deere 1025R price, and what it actually costs

Deere does not publish an MSRP the way Kubota does, and dealers quote a configured package rather than a machine. What is on record is the original list price by year, and the trend in it is worth seeing:

Year Original price
2013 $13,361
2019 $14,301
2026 $18,013

That is 35% over thirteen years, and 26% of it arrived after 2019. Two practical consequences. A used 1025R holds value unusually well, because the new one keeps moving away from it — which is the argument for checking the used value calculator before assuming new is the better deal. And the number you are quoted will be well above $18,013 once a loader and a deck are on it, because those are the parts of the package with the margin in them.

John Deere 1025R attachments, and what the hitch allows

The factory list is a 54 or 60 inch mid-mount mower deck, a front-end loader, a backhoe, a blade and a snowblower. Everything on it is either carried by the loader or driven from the mid PTO — none of it tests the rear hitch.

That is the pattern to notice. Rear implements have to live inside 681 lb and 18 PTO hp, and that pair rules out the two tools people most often ask about: a five-foot rotary cutter and a rotary tiller. The implement matcher checks a specific implement against both limits, and a box blade at this size has to be the light 48 inch version if it is going to be lifted at all.

Where it sits against the alternatives

Inside Deere's own range, the step down is the 1023E at 22.4 hp, and the step up is the 2025R, which keeps the same 18 PTO hp but lifts 1,150 lb — a meaningful difference if the hitch is the constraint.

Across brands, the machine people actually cross-shop is the Kubota B2601: 24.3 hp at the engine, 19.5 at the PTO, and 1,411 lb of lift, more than double the Deere. The trade is dealer coverage, where Deere is unmatched, and the mid-mount deck, which is what a lot of buyers are really shopping for.

The comparison people ask about but should not make is the L2502. It is a compact, not a sub-compact, and the numbers are not close.

What we could not verify

Deere's site blocks our checks, so every figure on this page comes from TractorData rather than from the manufacturer, and is labelled that way in the table above. We have not confirmed the loader lift capacity at all: TractorData lists the loader as an option without publishing a figure, so rather than repeat a number from a forum we leave it blank.

FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

How much does a John Deere 1025R cost?

Deere does not publish a list price the way Kubota does, and dealers quote packages rather than machines. The last figure recorded by TractorData for this model is $18,013 for 2026, against $14,301 in 2019 and $13,361 at launch in 2013 — a third more in thirteen years. What you actually pay depends on the loader, the deck and the dealer, and the tractor alone is the smallest part of that number.

What are the 1025R specs that actually matter?

23.8 hp at the engine, 18 hp at the PTO, a Category I hitch that lifts 681 lb at 24 inches behind it, 3.5 GPM of implement flow out of a 6.3 GPM pump, and 1,444 lb of machine. The two figures that decide what it can do are the lift and the flow, not the engine rating.

How much does a 1025R weigh?

1,444 lb bare, on a 57.1 inch wheelbase. That matters more than it sounds — weight is what keeps a loaded loader on the ground, and this is a light machine. Owners running the loader hard add rear ballast rather than argue about horsepower.

What attachments fit a 1025R?

A 54 or 60 inch mid-mount mower deck, a front-end loader, a backhoe, a blade and a snowblower are the factory list. Anything on the three-point hitch has to respect 681 lb and 18 PTO hp — that rules out most rotary tillers and full-size rotary cutters, which is the single most common mismatch in this class.

Is the 1025R being replaced?

TractorData lists production as 2013 to 2026 and shows a 1M series arriving for 2027. A model at the end of its cycle is the one dealers are most willing to move, so it is worth asking what is left on the lot rather than ordering.

1025R or Kubota L2502?

They are not in the same class, even though the engine ratings are two tenths apart. The 1025R lifts 681 lb and moves 3.5 GPM; the L2502 lifts 1,389 lb and moves 5.2 GPM. The Deere is a groundskeeping machine with a mid-mount deck; the Kubota is a working tractor with no deck option. Pick by which of those two jobs is yours.
