John Deere compact tractors, model by model

Updated 17 Aug 2026 · 8 min read
Short answer

Three John Deere models cover 1 to 20 acres — the 1025R, the 2025R and the 3025E. All three sit at 23.8 to 24.4 gross horsepower, deliberately under the 25 hp emissions threshold, and they differ where it matters — hitch capacity runs 681, 1,150 and 1,356 lb, and the 3025E has no mid PTO, so no mid-mount mower deck.

John Deere sells three machines into the one-to-twenty-acre range, and the specification sheets make them look like a neat ladder: 1025R, 2025R, 3025E, rising by roughly half a horsepower a step. The ladder is real, but the rungs are not where the model numbers put them.

Read the horsepower column and the three machines are the same tractor. Read the lift column and the top one does twice the work of the bottom one. That is the whole guide in two sentences.

Why they all stop at 24 horsepower

Not engineering — regulation. US EPA nonroad standards split at 19 kW, which is 25 hp. Under the line the particulate limit is 0.4 g/kWh; at or above it, 0.03 g/kWh. Thirteen times stricter, and it brings aftertreatment hardware with it — a diesel particulate filter, a regeneration cycle, and a machine that occasionally decides to burn off soot while you wanted to be working.

Every manufacturer in this class parks its models just under that line. It is why the whole segment clusters at 23 to 24.8 hp, and it is the single most useful thing to understand about compact tractor specifications.

1025R: the one people actually buy

The best-selling sub-compact in the country, and the one with the number that limits it — 681 lb at the hitch. TractorData's own entry calls the Category I hitch limited, which is unusually direct for a reference source.

It takes a mid-mount deck, it fits in a normal garage, and the loader handles what a household throws at it. What it does not do is heavy three-point work. A tiller sized for 24 horsepower will attach and then ask the hitch for more than it has.

Price history is worth a look on this one: $13,361 at launch in 2013 against $18,013 in 2026. That is a 35% rise over thirteen years on the same model line.

2025R: the same engine, a different machine

Same 18 hp claimed at the PTO. Same Yanmar 1.3L. And 1,150 lb at the hitch instead of 681, plus 216 lb of extra machine underneath it.

If you have looked at a 1025R and hesitated over the lift figure, this is the answer to that hesitation, and it costs the difference in frame rather than in power. No current list price is published to us — the last figure we can source is $15,348, from 2014, which is old enough to be a historical note rather than a guide.

3025E: more tractor, one missing part

The heaviest of the three at 2,222 lb, with the biggest hitch at 1,356 lb and half again the hydraulic flow — 5.3 GPM against 3.5. On the numbers it is the working machine of the range.

And it has no mid PTO listed, which means no mid-mount mower deck. If mowing is on your job list, that single line removes the 3025E from consideration regardless of everything above it. It also gives up PTO power to the smaller machines — 17.4 hp against 18 — because the bigger engine is doing more work to move a heavier tractor.

Where a Kubota does more

Worth stating plainly, because it is the comparison every buyer in this class actually makes. A Kubota L2502 puts 19.8 hp on the PTO from a 23.3 hp engine and lifts 1,389 lb, against the 3025E's 17.4 hp and 1,356 lb from a larger engine. On implement power per horsepower, the Kubota wins.

What Deere brings is the dealer network, and that is not a soft factor. Over ten years the thing that decides whether a machine keeps working is how far away parts and service are — and on that measure Deere's coverage in most of the US is the widest in the industry.

What we could not verify

Everything on this page is TractorData, not John Deere. Deere's own site does not serve our checks, so we have no manufacturer specification sheet and no current MSRP for two of the three machines. Where a price appears it carries the year it belongs to.

If you are choosing between these and a competitor, treat our figures as directionally right and confirm the two numbers that decide your job — hitch capacity and PTO horsepower — against a current dealer quote before you sign anything.

FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

Which John Deere compact tractor should I buy?

Decide on the hitch, not the badge. The 1025R lifts 681 lb and takes a mid-mount deck; the 2025R lifts 1,150 lb on the same engine and PTO rating; the 3025E lifts 1,356 lb and moves half again the hydraulic flow but cannot take a mid-mount mower. If you are mowing, that last point rules the 3025E out before any other comparison starts.

What is the difference between the 1025R and the 2025R?

On paper the engines are near-identical — 23.8 against 24.2 gross horsepower, both with the same 18 hp claimed at the PTO. The 2025R lifts 1,150 lb against 681, weighs 216 lb more, and carries a heavier frame. You are paying for the hitch and the mass, not for engine power.

Why do John Deere compact tractors stop at about 24 horsepower?

The US EPA nonroad rules split at 19 kW, which is 25 hp. Below the line the particulate limit is 0.4 g/kWh; at or above it the limit is 0.03 — thirteen times stricter, and it brings aftertreatment with it. Every manufacturer in this class parks models just under the threshold, which is why the whole segment clusters at 23 to 24.8 hp.

Does John Deere publish prices for these machines?

Not to us. Deere's own site does not serve our checks, and dealers in this industry sell through a quote rather than a listed price. The figures on this page come from TractorData's reference entries and are labelled with the year they belong to — the 1025R's $18,013 is a 2026 figure, the 3025E's $18,330 is from 2020.

How does a John Deere compare with a Kubota of the same size?

On PTO power the Kubota does more with less engine — an L2502 puts 19.8 hp on the shaft from 23.3 gross, while a 3025E puts 17.4 from 24.4. On lift the L2502 does 1,389 lb against the 3025E's 1,356. What Deere brings is dealer coverage, which is a real specification over ten years of ownership.

Is the 1025R's 681 lb hitch enough?

For a box blade, a rotary cutter of the right width and light three-point work, yes. For a tiller, a heavy blade or anything at the top of the Category I range it is the limiting number on the machine, and it is the reason people move up to the 2025R.
