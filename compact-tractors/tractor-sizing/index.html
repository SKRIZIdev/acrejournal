How the answer is calculated

Acreage is the input people expect to matter and the one that matters least. What decides the machine is the heaviest job you will do repeatedly: the weight of the implement it has to lift, the flow a hydraulic attachment needs, and whether the ground is level.

That is why this tool asks what you do rather than how much land you have. Five acres of lawn and five acres of scrub with a gravel drive to clear are not the same problem, and no acreage figure separates them.

Where these numbers come from

Kubota figures in this tool are manufacturer MSRP and horsepower read from the Kubota USA series pages on 16 August 2026 and marked as verified. Other brands are estimates in the right class, marked unverified, because those manufacturers block our checks — and an estimate presented as a specification is worse than no number at all.

FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

Does more horsepower always help?

Past a point, weight and hydraulics matter more than engine power — a heavier machine with the same horsepower does more work.

Is a sub-compact enough for five acres?

It depends far more on the heaviest implement you plan to run than on the acreage itself.
