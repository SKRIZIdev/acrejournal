The 3025E is Deere's entry into the compact class proper, and the machine most often put opposite a Kubota L2502 on a showroom floor. It is bigger than it looks on paper: 2,222 lb of tractor, a 1.6L engine, and 9.3 GPM of pump capacity.

It also loses more power on the way to the implement than anything else we have measured in this class.

The number that decides it

24.4 hp at the engine, 17.4 hp at the PTO. Seven horsepower disappears between the flywheel and the shaft — normal in principle, unusually large in practice for this size of machine.

The comparison that matters is the L2502, because these two are cross-shopped constantly. Kubota starts from a smaller engine at 23.3 hp and still puts 19.8 hp on the PTO in gear-drive form. That is 2.4 hp more implement power from 1.1 hp less engine.

Engine horsepower is what the brochure leads with. PTO horsepower is what runs the tiller.

What this machine is for

Loader work, land work, moving material, running rear implements within 17.4 hp. Its real advantage is mass: at 2,222 lb it holds a loaded loader down in a way the sub-compacts cannot, and it does not need a ballast box to be usable.

What it will not do is mow a lawn from a mid-mount deck, because there is no mid PTO to drive one. That single omission moves this machine out of the running for a large share of buyers, and it is not obvious from the marketing.

John Deere 3025E price, as far as it is on record

Deere publishes no MSRP to us. TractorData records $17,324 for 2017 and $18,330 for 2020, which is the most recent figure on file — six years old at the time of writing.

The useful comparison is against the machine it is cross-shopped with. A Kubota L2502 lists today at $17,449, published by the manufacturer, and that price is checkable. When one side of a comparison publishes a number and the other does not, the negotiation starts from the published one — bring it to the conversation.

Where it sits against the alternatives

Against the L2502 it wins on weight and total pump capacity and loses on PTO power and on list price transparency — Kubota publishes an MSRP, Deere does not. Against its own 2025R it is a class up: 1,356 lb of lift against 1,150, and 2,222 lb of machine against 1,660, at the cost of the mid-mount deck.

The full side-by-side, including what we could not confirm, is on the L2502 vs 3025E page.

What we could not verify

Everything here is TractorData rather than Deere: their site does not serve our checks, and we do not write around site protection. Loader lift capacity is not published by the source, so we leave it out rather than repeat a forum figure. There is no current price.

FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

What are the John Deere 3025E specifications?

24.4 hp at the engine and 17.4 hp at the PTO, a Category I hitch lifting 1,356 lb at 24 inches behind it, 5.3 GPM of implement flow out of 9.3 GPM total, a Yanmar 1.6L three-cylinder diesel, hydrostatic drive and 2,222 lb of machine.

Why does a bigger engine send less power to the PTO than a Kubota L2502?

Because the two figures are measured in different places and lose different amounts on the way. The 3025E is rated 24.4 hp at the engine and 17.4 hp at the PTO — a loss of seven horsepower. The L2502 starts lower, at 23.3 hp, and still delivers 19.8 hp to the PTO in gear-drive form. If what you are buying is implement power, the smaller engine is the stronger machine.

Does the 3025E take a mid-mount mower deck?

No. TractorData lists no mid PTO for this model, and a mid-mount deck is driven from one. The factory attachment list is a front-end loader and a backhoe. If mowing a lawn is the main job, this is the wrong machine and a 2025R or a Kubota BX is the right one.

How much does a 3025E cost?

No current list price is published to us. The most recent original price recorded by TractorData is $18,330 for 2020, against $17,324 in 2017. Dealers in this class sell a configured package rather than a machine, so the tractor's own price is only part of the number you will be quoted.

Is it heavy enough for loader work?

At 2,222 lb it is the heaviest machine on our shortlist and considerably heavier than the sub-compacts, which is an advantage here — mass is what holds a loaded bucket down. This is the practical argument for stepping up from a 2025R.
