FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

Where do these explainers get their numbers?

Manufacturer specification pages, university extension publications and public datasets such as the USDA soil survey. Every figure carries a source and the date it was checked, and where we could not read a figure at source we say so on the page rather than repeating someone else's transcription.

How often are they updated?

When the underlying facts move — a new model generation, a regulatory change, a revised price list. Each page shows when it was last touched, so a stale page is visible rather than disguised.

Why are the answers so short?

Because most of these questions have short answers, and padding them out to look thorough wastes your time. Where a subject genuinely needs length, it gets a section of its own rather than a longer explainer.
