What the numbers assume

The interval this tool returns comes from your own ground, not from an average. It takes the ZIP code, finds the mapped soil series underneath it in the USDA soil survey, reads the water holding capacity for that series and multiplies by rooting depth to get the reservoir the crop can actually draw on.

Two limits are worth stating plainly. The survey does not map every location — city centers and institutional ZIP codes sometimes have no soil data, and the tool then samples nearby points. And it models the soil side only: your weather, wind and water quality are not in it. Treat the result as the starting interval and adjust from what the soil tells you at rooting depth.

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

How long does drip tape last?

Thin-wall tape is usually a season or two; thicker wall and buried lines last longer.

Do I need a pressure regulator?

Drip components are rated for low pressure, and household supply is usually far above it.
