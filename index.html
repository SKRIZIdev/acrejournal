FAQ

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

What is this site for?

Working out what to buy for one to twenty acres, and what it will actually do once it arrives. The specification sheets in this industry are accurate and misleading at the same time — engine horsepower is not what reaches the implement, a rated lift is measured at a distance you will not use, and the price shown is rarely the price you pay. We publish the numbers that survive contact with the field.

Where do the numbers come from?

Manufacturers, university extension services and public datasets — each with a link and the date we checked it. Prices are read from the seller on the date shown. Where a manufacturer blocks our access, as John Deere does, the page marks the figure unverified rather than restating someone else's transcription.

Do you test equipment yourselves?

No, and we will not write as though we had. Owner reports here are built by counting what owners discuss and linking to the source, with the sample size printed on the page. If we get hands-on with equipment — realistic at greenhouse and hydroponics scale — that page will say exactly what we did.

How do you make money?

Some links to sellers earn a commission, marked as sponsored with the disclosure next to the block rather than buried in the footer. No paid placements, no sponsored articles, no ad networks. Several product notes here tell you to buy the cheaper option or not to buy at all.

How often is it updated?

When the facts move. Model generations change — the Kubota L2501 became the L2502 — prices drift, and regulations shift, as the July 2026 right-to-repair settlement did. Every page shows when it was last touched, so a stale page is visible rather than disguised.
