Affiliate disclosure

Updated 16 Aug 2026

Some links on this site earn us money. This page explains exactly which ones, how the arrangement works, and what it does and does not affect. If you would rather not read the whole thing: it costs you nothing, and it does not decide what we recommend.

What earns us money

Links to sellers. Where we point at a product, that link may carry a tracking parameter. If you buy after following it, the seller pays us a share of the sale or a fixed fee. The price you pay is the same either way — commission comes out of the seller's margin, not out of your pocket.

Links to financing. Enquiry forms for equipment financing pay a fixed amount per qualified application rather than a percentage of anything. That means we are paid the same whether you borrow $5,000 or $50,000, and whether the loan completes or not.

Every link of either kind is marked in the page source with rel="sponsored nofollow", and the block it sits in carries a disclosure line next to it — not buried in this page alone.

What does not earn us money

  • No paid placement. No manufacturer, dealer or seller has paid to appear on this site, to be recommended, or to be described favourably. Nobody has been offered that option.
  • No sponsored articles. Nothing here was written for a fee by or for a company in this industry.
  • No display advertising. There are no ad networks on this site.
  • No paid reviews. We do not accept payment for coverage of a product.

What this changes about our recommendations

Nothing, and there is a structural reason rather than only a promise.

The products we can earn from are a small subset of what this site covers. Tractors — the core of the site — have no affiliate programmes at all: the industry sells through dealers and quotes. Greenhouse manufacturers largely have none either. If commission decided our coverage, we would write about grow lights and drip tape and nothing else, because that is where the programmes are.

Where we do link to a seller, we say what the product is for and who should not buy it. Several product notes on this site advise readers to buy the cheaper option, or to hire rather than buy, or to skip an item entirely. Those notes cost us money and stay in.

Prices

Prices shown on this site were read from the seller on the date printed next to them. They change without notice and they exclude shipping and tax unless stated. Always check the current price at the seller before deciding anything — a figure from last month is context, not a quote.

Reviews and owner reports

We do not publish first-person reviews of equipment we have not used. Where a page aggregates what owners report, it says how many reports were reviewed, over what period, and where they came from — and it does not reproduce the text of other people's posts.

Aggregate ratings appear in our page markup only when we have both a rating and a sample size of our own. We do not pass off other sites' review scores as ours.

Questions

If something on this site looks like it was written for a commission rather than for you, that is worth telling us about — the about page explains how we work and how to raise it.
