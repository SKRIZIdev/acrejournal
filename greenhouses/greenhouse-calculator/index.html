What the numbers assume

The tool checks one thing catalogues never flag: whether the covering you picked can carry the snow you specified over the span you chose. That combination is where tunnels fail, and it is a combination — not any single choice.

Local ground snow load governs, and it comes from your county's building code rather than from a manufacturer's brochure. Commercial designs are commonly rated to around 35 lb per square foot, but that is a manufacturing convention, not a statement about your site.

Questions & Answers

Answered from the same sources as the tables above — each figure is linked on the page it comes from.

Polycarbonate or film?

Film is cheapest and gets replaced every few seasons; twin-wall holds snow and heat far better.

Does the kit include a foundation?

Almost never — budget for ground anchors or a base separately, along with end walls and a door.
