THE WORDS ON THE SPEC SHEET

24 terms

Every term used across this site, defined once — and linked automatically the first time it appears on any page.

Spec sheets and dealer brochures assume you already know the vocabulary. This page defines it once — and every article links the first mention of a term straight here.

Ballast

Added rear weight that keeps the tractor stable when the loader is full.

Box blade

Rear implement for grading and moving soil, with scarifiers for hard ground.

Category 1

Hitch size class covering most compact and utility tractors.

Center pivot

Rotating sprinkler system that waters a circular field from a central point.

Compact tractor

Roughly 20 to 45 hp with a category one hitch — the core class of this site.

Drawbar

Rear towing point for trailed implements, distinct from the three-point hitch.

Drip tape

Thin-walled tubing with built-in emitters, laid along a crop row.

Emitter

Outlet that meters water to a plant at a set flow rate.

Gear drive

Mechanical transmission with fixed ratios — simpler and cheaper than hydrostatic.

High tunnel

Unheated covered structure grown in directly at ground level.

Hydraulic flow

Gallons per minute available to attachments — decides how fast a loader cycles.

Hydrostatic transmission

Fluid drive with no clutch pedal — easier for loader work, slightly less efficient.

Loader lift capacity

Weight the front loader raises, measured at the pivot pins.

Polycarbonate panel

Twin-wall greenhouse glazing — lighter than glass, warmer than film.

Pressure regulator

Drops line pressure to what drip components are rated for.

PTO

Splined shaft that sends engine power to an implement instead of the wheels.

Quick attach

Standard loader mount that lets one machine take many attachments.

ROPS

Rollover protective structure — the frame that keeps the operator alive in a tip.

Rotary cutter

Heavy mower for tall grass and brush, driven from the PTO.

Snow load

Weight of snow a greenhouse frame is rated to carry, in pounds per square foot.

Sub-compact tractor

Smallest class, roughly under 25 hp, sized for lawns and small acreage.

Three-point hitch

The standard mounting linkage at the rear of a tractor, sized by category.

Three-point lift capacity

Weight the rear hitch raises — the number that limits which implements you can run.

Utility tractor

Larger class above compacts, from about 45 hp, for real field work.
